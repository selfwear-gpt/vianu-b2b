TEMPLATE: WORKS AGREEMENT FOR THE PILOT WITH VIANU
Status: draft. Not legal advice.

How to use this
This text is a starting point for the discussion with your works council. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Have your legal counsel and your data protection officer review it before you use it. Passages in square brackets are placeholders. They must be completed by your company or confirmed by VIANU. This text does not say whether the pilot needs a works agreement. Clarify that with your legal counsel.

1. Purpose
The Employer runs a pilot with custom-made insoles from VIANU. The pilot should show whether the insoles make a difference for the employees who take part. The insoles are a supplementary comfort offer. They do not replace any protective measure or workplace design.

2. Scope
This agreement applies to [Placeholder: site, department or group] and to up to [Placeholder: number] employees. The pilot is planned for 10 employees.

3. Voluntary participation
Taking part is voluntary. Anyone who does not take part has no disadvantages. Anyone who takes part can end their participation. [Placeholder: rule on withdrawal and on data already collected, to be reviewed legally.]

4. Process
a) The Employer decides which employees can take part.
b) The employees who take part photograph their feet with their own smartphone during a break. There is no measurement day and there are no additional devices.
c) VIANU makes a custom-made insole for each person who takes part. [Placeholder: engraving of name and company logo, to be confirmed by VIANU.]
d) The insole reaches the employees at the workplace, at the latest 7 days after the foot capture. [Placeholder: evidence from VIANU for the delivery time.]
e) The insole goes into shoes the employees already own.
f) The employees pay nothing. The Employer bears the cost.
