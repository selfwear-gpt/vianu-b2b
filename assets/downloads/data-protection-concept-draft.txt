DATA PROTECTION CONCEPT FOR THE PILOT WITH VIANU
Status: draft. Not legal advice.

How to use this
This draft shows which points a data protection concept for the pilot must settle, and it enters what is known today. Everything else is marked as open. The concept has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Have your data protection officer and your legal counsel review it. As long as points are open, VIANU makes no promise about them.

1. Purpose and scope
The pilot checks whether custom-made insoles make a difference for 10 employees who take part. This concept describes which data is created for that.

2. Which data is created
- Photos of the feet, taken with the participant's own smartphone during a break.
- The details needed to make the insole. [Placeholder: exact details, for example name and company logo for the engraving, to be confirmed by VIANU.]
- Four answers at the end of the pilot: wear rate, noticeable improvement, recommendation and appreciation. The participants answer them themselves.

3. Data flow
a) The participant takes the photos and releases them. Taking part is voluntary.
b) VIANU makes the insole.
c) The insole reaches the person at the workplace.
d) At the end the employer receives an anonymized report at group level.
