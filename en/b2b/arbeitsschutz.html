Roderich to Occupational safety

Let me say it clearly up front: VIANU is a comfort offer. I make no claim about sick days, pain or protection, and the insole replaces no workplace design measure. It adds to what you already have, for example a mat in a fixed spot.

I stood on hard floors for 10 to 12 hours a day myself. What matters to me is that your team has an insole in the shoe along the whole route of a shift. On material, hygiene and data handling I will say something once it is settled.

Evidence

Two reviews on cushioning during standing and on foot complaints among nurses.

Speed et al. 2018

Cushioning during long standing at work

A review of 10 studies found moderate evidence that cushioned materials (softer floors, cushioned insoles) go along with less discomfort and fatigue during long standing at work. For insoles compared with mats, the evidence is limited.

Speed et al. 2018, Applied Ergonomics 70:300-314, doi:10.1016/j.apergo.2018.02.021

Bernardes et al. 2023

Foot and ankle disorders among nurses

A review of foot and ankle disorders among nurses in hospitals names pain as the most common disorder. The reported frequency differs clearly between the studies.

Bernardes et al. 2023, Workplace Health & Safety 71(3):101-116, doi:10.1177/21650799221137646

What we measure at the end

For occupational safety, noticeable improvement and wear rate matter most. At the end of the pilot, participants give all four answers themselves. All values are self-reported.

The pilot counts as a success if the limits of the product are clear to the participants, no avoidable objections on hygiene or data arise and the pilot is not read as a replacement for other protective measures.

These are values on comfort and acceptance. They say nothing about sick days, turnover, performance or pain.

Common questions

The most important

Do insoles replace anti-fatigue mats?

No. A mat lies in one fixed spot. An insole is in the shoe along the whole route of a shift. VIANU sees the insole as a complement to the mat and makes no comparison of effect.

Do insoles replace occupational safety measures at work?

No. VIANU offers the insole as a supplementary comfort offer. It replaces no protective measure and no workplace design.

More questions

Which employees in hospitality can take part in an insole program?

An insole program can include employees from kitchen, service, housekeeping, front desk and bar. At VIANU, taking part is voluntary, and your business decides whether to join.

Do employees have to buy new shoes for insoles?

No, an insole goes into shoes you already own. VIANU hands over the insole at your business, and it goes into the shoes your employees already have.

What is VIANU?

VIANU is a program for teams in hospitality that gives employees custom-made insoles. It consists of an approval package, the rollout at your business and a results report. The foot capture runs on their own smartphone during a break, with no measurement day.
