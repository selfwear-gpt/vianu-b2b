Anti-fatigue mat and insole: what helps where?

An anti-fatigue mat is a cushioned mat that lies at one fixed spot on the floor, for example at the stove. In many businesses it is a common measure.

  1. The matIt helps as long as someone stands on it. It stays where it lies.
  2. The routeA shift leads through kitchen, service, storage and cold room. A large part of it lies away from the mat.
  3. The insoleIt sits in the person's own shoe and is with them all day. It helps at every spot, also on the way between workplaces.

Mats cannot be laid at every spot employees pass through in a day. The insole goes with each person, at the stove, in storage and on the way in between. Whoever has mats already keeps them and gains the stretch in between.

Common questions

The most important

Do insoles replace anti-fatigue mats?

No. An anti-fatigue mat lies in one fixed place and helps only there. An insole is in the shoe along the whole route of a shift, all day. VIANU sees the insole as a complement to the anti-fatigue mat.

Do insoles replace occupational safety measures at work?

No. VIANU offers the insole as a supplementary comfort offer. It replaces no protective measure and no workplace design.

What material and hygiene information is there for the insole?

The insole is made by 3D printing locally in Germany. Before you order, you receive a data sheet on material and hygiene.

Who sees the photos from the foot scan?

Only VIANU and its manufacturing partners see the photos of the feet. They are used only to make the insole, processed in the EU and deleted afterwards.

More questions

Which employees can take part in an insole program?

An insole program suits all employees who stand or walk for many hours a day. The focus is on work in hotels and restaurants, such as kitchen, service, housekeeping, reception, banquet and bar. All other industries with a lot of standing and walking are just as welcome, for example care, retail, production, logistics and cleaning. At VIANU, taking part is voluntary, and your business decides whether to join.

Do employees have to buy new shoes for insoles?

No, an insole goes into shoes you already own. The insole reaches your employees at your business and goes into the shoes they already have.

What is VIANU?

VIANU is a program for teams whose staff are on their feet for many hours a day. Employees get custom-made insoles. VIANU is a comfort and appreciation offer that starts as a limited pilot. It consists of an approval package, the rollout at your business and a results report. The foot capture runs on their own smartphone, for example during a break, with no measurement day.

How is a custom insole made?

A custom insole can be made with 3D printing from the photos of the feet. At VIANU, it is printed locally in Germany and made within 48 hours. It reaches your employees at the latest 7 days after the foot capture.

How long does an insole last in everyday work?

How long an insole lasts in everyday work depends on the provider. At VIANU, we know from our own tests that the insole holds up in perfect condition for at least 3 years.

How long does it take from the foot scan to delivery of the insoles?

How long it takes from the foot capture to delivery depends on the provider. At VIANU, the insole reaches your employees at the latest 7 days after the foot capture.
