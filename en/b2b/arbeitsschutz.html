Evidence

Two reviews on cushioning during standing and on foot complaints among nurses.

Speed et al. 2018

Cushioning during long standing at work

A review of 10 studies found moderate evidence that cushioned materials (softer floors, cushioned insoles) go along with less discomfort and fatigue during long standing at work. For insoles compared with anti-fatigue mats, the evidence is limited.

Speed et al. 2018, Applied Ergonomics 70:300-314, doi:10.1016/j.apergo.2018.02.021

Bernardes et al. 2023

Foot and ankle disorders among nurses

A review of foot and ankle disorders among nurses in hospitals names pain as the most common disorder. The reported frequency differs clearly between the studies.

Bernardes et al. 2023, Workplace Health & Safety 71(3):101-116, doi:10.1177/21650799221137646

Common questions

The most important

Do insoles replace anti-fatigue mats?

No. An anti-fatigue mat lies in one fixed spot. An insole is in the shoe along the whole route of a shift. VIANU sees the insole as a complement to the anti-fatigue mat and makes no comparison of effect.

Do insoles replace occupational safety measures at work?

No. VIANU offers the insole as a supplementary comfort offer. It replaces no protective measure and no workplace design.

More questions

Which employees can take part in an insole program?

An insole program can include employees from kitchen, service, housekeeping, reception and bar. At VIANU, taking part is voluntary, and your business decides whether to join.

Do employees have to buy new shoes for insoles?

No, an insole goes into shoes you already own. The insole reaches your employees at your business and goes into the shoes they already have.

What is VIANU?

VIANU is a program for teams whose staff are on their feet for many hours a day. Employees get custom-made insoles. It consists of an approval package, the rollout at your business and a results report. The foot capture runs on their own smartphone during a break, with no measurement day.

How long does an insole last in everyday work?

That depends on the provider. At VIANU, we know from our own tests that the insole holds up in perfect condition for at least 3 years.

How long does it take from the foot scan to delivery of the insoles?

That depends on the provider. At VIANU, the insole reaches your employees at the latest 7 days after the foot capture.
