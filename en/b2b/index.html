FAQ

Insoles for teams

How much does a pilot with insoles for 10 employees cost?

What a pilot with insoles for 10 employees costs depends on the provider. At VIANU, a pair costs EUR 120, plus VAT, so the pilot with 10 pairs costs EUR 1,200, plus VAT. The regular price applies.

How does a hotel introduce insoles for its team?

A hotel can introduce insoles first with a limited test group and then decide whether and how to expand. At VIANU, this is the pilot with 10 employees, and you do not commit to a rollout beforehand.

Who pays for insoles for employees, the business or the staff?

Who pays for insoles for employees depends on the business. At VIANU, the employer pays for the program, and there is no cost for the employees.

Does an insole replace anti-fatigue mats?

No. A mat lies in one fixed place, while an insole is in the shoe along the whole route of a shift. VIANU sees the insole as a complement to the mat and makes no comparison of effect.

Which staff in hospitality and food service can take part in an insole program?

An insole program can include staff from the kitchen, service, housekeeping, reception and bar. At VIANU, taking part is voluntary, and your business decides whether to join.

Do employees have to buy new shoes for insoles?

No, an insole goes into shoes you already own. VIANU hands over the insole at your business, and it goes into the shoes your employees already have.

About VIANU

What is VIANU?

VIANU is a program for teams in hospitality and food service in which employees get custom-made insoles. It consists of an approval package, the rollout at your business and a results report. The foot capture runs on the employee's own smartphone during a break, with no measurement day.

How does a pilot with VIANU work?

A pilot with VIANU runs in four steps: you sign up your team, your employees do the foot capture on their own smartphone during a break, VIANU makes the insoles, and you hand them over at your business. The details of the process are not yet fixed.
