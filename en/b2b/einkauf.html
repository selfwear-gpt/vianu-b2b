Common questions

The most important

What does an insole pilot for 10 employees cost?

What a pilot with insoles for 10 employees costs depends on the provider. At VIANU a pair costs EUR 120 plus VAT, so the pilot with 10 pairs costs EUR 1,200 plus VAT. The regular price applies.

Are there minimum quantities for insoles at a business?

Minimum quantities for insoles differ by provider. At VIANU the pilot with 10 pairs is the first test. Afterwards you decide whether a larger rollout is worthwhile. We do not supply individuals.

How does ordering an insole pilot work?

Ordering an insole pilot works like this at VIANU: you send a request, we send the offer by email, and where needed there is a video meeting first. Employees scan their feet in a few minutes, then the order goes out to us. Payment is by invoice with 14 days to pay. The insoles reach the company no later than 7 days after the foot scan.

Who is liable for complaints about insoles for employees?

At VIANU all insoles come with the satisfaction guarantee. If an insole does not fit or the person is not satisfied, we talk to them personally online and rework the insole or make a new one. It reaches the company free of charge. If the person is still not satisfied afterwards, we refund the purchase price in full.

Which questions and special cases arise when procuring insoles for several sites?

When procuring insoles for several sites, questions arise mainly about minimum quantities, returns, delivery times and branding. A pilot with a fixed test group and a documented ordering route keeps them small. At VIANU, the price of EUR 120 per pair, plus VAT, and the delivery time of at most 7 days after the foot capture are settled, while minimum quantities, complaints process and delivery and service terms are not yet.

More questions

How is a custom insole made?

A custom insole can be made with 3D printing from the photos of the feet. At VIANU, it is printed locally in Germany and made within 48 hours. It reaches your employees at the latest 7 days after the foot capture. For the method of measuring feet from photos, patent DE 10 2019 123 458 B4 has been granted in Germany.

How long does it take from the foot scan to delivery of the insoles?

The delivery time of insoles after the foot capture differs by provider. At VIANU, the insole reaches your employees at the latest 7 days after the foot capture.

How long does an insole last in everyday work?

How long an insole lasts differs by provider. At VIANU, we know from our own tests that the insole holds up in perfect condition for at least 3 years.

Can we put our company logo on insoles for employees?

Whether insoles can carry a company logo depends on the provider. At VIANU the name and company logo are printed on the insole.

Who pays for insoles for employees, the business or the employees?

Who pays for insoles for employees depends on the business. At VIANU the employer pays for the program, and employees pay nothing.

What is VIANU?

VIANU is a program for teams whose staff are on their feet for many hours a day. Employees get custom-made insoles. VIANU is a comfort and appreciation offer that starts as a limited pilot and consists of an approval package, the rollout at your business and a results report. The foot capture runs on their own smartphone, for example during a break, with no measurement day. For the method of measuring feet from photos, patent DE 10 2019 123 458 B4 has been granted in Germany.
