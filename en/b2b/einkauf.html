What you need to be able to stand behind in the meeting

1The role's question

Is it standardized, clearly priced and easy to procure?

2Core message

“Easy to procure. Rolled out uniformly. Visibly on brand.”

3How this page sees it

The pilot needs a defined test group, a documented ordering route and clear statements on price, quantities, design and service framework.

4The pilot counts as a success if

an order is possible without a special case, queries are answered clearly and the test group causes no unplanned effort at the sites.

5What is needed before approval

Price and quantity logic, design options, ordering route, complaints process and delivery and service terms that have been specifically approved.

Contact: Roderich. Book a pilot on the VIANU B2B page.

What we measure at the end

For procurement, wear rate and recommendation matter most. At the end of the pilot, participants give all four answers themselves.

  • Wear rate

    “Are you still wearing the insoles?”

  • Recommendation

    “Would you recommend them?”

  • Noticeable improvement

    “Do you notice a difference compared to before?”

  • Appreciation

    “Do you feel valued by this offer?”

Who pays for insoles for employees, the business or the employees?

Who pays for insoles for employees depends on the business. At VIANU the employer pays for the program, and employees pay nothing.

What is VIANU?

VIANU is a program for teams whose staff are on their feet for many hours a day. Employees get custom-made insoles. It consists of an approval package, the rollout at your business and a results report. The foot capture runs on their own smartphone during a break, with no measurement day.
