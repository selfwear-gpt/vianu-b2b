Which questions and special cases arise when procuring insoles for several sites?

When procuring insoles for several sites, questions arise mainly about minimum quantities, returns, delivery times and branding. A pilot with a fixed test group and a documented ordering route keeps them small. At VIANU, the price of EUR 120 per pair, plus VAT, and the delivery time of at most 7 days after the foot capture are settled, while minimum quantities, complaints process and delivery and service terms are not yet.

More questions

How long does it take from the foot scan to delivery of the insoles?

The delivery time of insoles after the foot capture differs by provider. At VIANU, the insole reaches your employees at the latest 7 days after the foot capture.

How long does an insole last in everyday work?

How long an insole lasts differs by provider. At VIANU, we know from our own tests that the insole holds up in perfect condition for at least 3 years.

Who pays for insoles for employees, the business or the employees?

Who pays for insoles for employees depends on the business. At VIANU the employer pays for the program, and employees pay nothing.

What is VIANU?

VIANU is a program for teams whose staff are on their feet for many hours a day. Employees get custom-made insoles. VIANU is a comfort and appreciation offer that starts as a limited pilot and consists of an approval package, the rollout at your business and a results report. The foot capture runs on their own smartphone, for example during a break, with no measurement day.
