Frequently asked questions

The essentials

What does an insole pilot for 10 employees cost?

What a pilot with insoles for 10 employees costs depends on the provider. At VIANU, a pair costs EUR 120, plus VAT, so the pilot with 10 pairs costs EUR 1,200, plus VAT, and the regular price applies.

Is VIANU trustworthy and who is behind it?

VIANU was built by its founder Roderich Körner-Rehn, who is your contact and replies within 5 working days. The insoles are 3D-printed locally in Germany. For the method of measuring feet from photos, patent DE 10 2019 123 458 B4 has been granted in Germany. The pilot with 10 pairs has a fixed price of EUR 1,200 plus VAT, and the satisfaction guarantee applies to all insoles.

What if the budget is tight this quarter?

With a tight budget, a pilot can be kept small. At VIANU, that means 10 pairs at EUR 120 per pair, EUR 1,200 in total, plus VAT. After that, you decide whether and how to expand, and you do not commit to a rollout beforehand.

Can an insole pilot be limited to a test group?

Yes, a pilot can be limited to a test group. At VIANU, that is 10 employees whom your company selects, and participation is voluntary.

How much time does an insole pilot cost a business in everyday shifts?

Per employee, a few minutes for the foot capture with a smartphone. It can be done, for example, in a short break. After that, it is our turn, and you receive the insoles within 7 days.

Is an insole pilot more than a nice extra for a business?

Whether an insole pilot is more than a nice extra for a business can only be checked in the team itself, for example with a small test group and questions to the participants after the test. At VIANU, you get a report at the end of the pilot on how helpful the insoles were and how satisfied the employees are with the measure.

More questions

Who pays for insoles for employees, the company or the staff?

Who pays for insoles for employees depends on the company. At VIANU, the employer pays for the program, and employees incur no costs.

How long does it take from the foot scan to delivery of the insoles?

How long it takes from the foot capture to delivery depends on the provider. At VIANU, the insole reaches your employees at the latest 7 days after the foot capture.

How long does an insole last in everyday work?

How long an insole lasts in everyday work depends on the provider. At VIANU, we know from our own tests that the insole holds up in perfect condition for at least 3 years.
