Evidence

One study on missed work hours when working standing up.

Smith et al. 2023

Work hours missed due to pain

In a UK online survey of 1,035 workers with lower-body pain from standing work, respondents reported an average of 4.12 work hours missed per week due to pain. The figures are self-reported and contain no costs. The study was funded by an insole manufacturer.

Smith et al. 2023, BMC Research Notes 16:186, doi:10.1186/s13104-023-06461-5

Frequently asked questions

The essentials

What does an insole pilot for 10 employees cost?

What a pilot with insoles for 10 employees costs depends on the provider. At VIANU, a pair costs EUR 120, plus VAT, so the pilot with 10 pairs costs EUR 1,200, plus VAT, and the regular price applies.

What if the budget is tight this quarter?

With a tight budget, a pilot can be kept small. At VIANU, that means 10 pairs at EUR 120 per pair, EUR 1,200 in total, plus VAT. After that, you decide whether and how to expand, and you do not commit to a rollout beforehand.

Can an insole pilot be limited to a test group?

Yes, a pilot can be limited to a test group. At VIANU, that is 10 employees whom your company selects, and participation is voluntary.

More questions

Who pays for insoles for employees, the company or the staff?

Who pays for insoles for employees depends on the company. At VIANU, the employer pays for the program, and employees incur no costs.

Does VIANU make any statement about sick days, turnover or performance?

No. VIANU makes no statement on sick days, turnover, performance or pain. At the end of the pilot, participants report themselves whether they wear the insoles, notice a difference, would recommend them and feel valued.
