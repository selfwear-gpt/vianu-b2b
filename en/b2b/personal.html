Evidence

Two studies on nurses, a profession with a lot of standing and walking.

Reed et al. 2014

Foot and ankle complaints among nurses

In a survey of 304 nurses at a children's hospital in Australia, 55.3% reported foot or ankle complaints within twelve months. After the lower back and the neck, it was the third most common body region. The figure is self-reported and applies to this group.

Reed et al. 2014, BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders 15:196, doi:10.1186/1471-2474-15-196

Bernardes et al. 2023

Foot and ankle disorders among nurses

A review of foot and ankle disorders among nurses in hospitals names pain as the most common disorder. The reported frequency differs markedly between the studies.

Bernardes et al. 2023, Workplace Health & Safety 71(3):101-116, doi:10.1177/21650799221137646

Frequently asked questions

The essentials

How does a company introduce a new employee benefit such as insoles without it fizzling out?

A company can first introduce a new benefit with a limited test group and then decide whether and how to expand it. At VIANU, that is the pilot with 10 employees, and the results report shows at the end whether your team wears the insole.

How can a company tell whether an employee benefit is landing with the team?

A company can ask participants about their experience. At VIANU, participants answer four questions themselves at the end of the pilot: whether they still wear the insoles, whether they notice a difference, whether they would recommend them and whether they feel valued. The results report shows the answers anonymized at group level, as self-reported.

What happens in a pilot if only part of the team takes part?

In a voluntary pilot, not everyone may take part. At VIANU, participation is voluntary, the wear rate is one of the four values at the end of the pilot, and how the report looks with fewer participants has not been decided yet.

More questions

Which employees can take part in an insole program?

An insole program can include employees from kitchen, service, housekeeping, reception and bar. At VIANU, participation is voluntary, and your company decides whether to take part.

How does a foot scan with a smartphone work?

In a foot scan with a smartphone, the feet are photographed with your own phone. No measurement day and no additional devices are needed. VIANU calls this foot capture: it takes place during a break, with no measurement day and no extra devices.

What is VIANU?

VIANU is a program for teams whose staff are on their feet for many hours a day. Employees get custom-made insoles. It consists of an approval package, implementation on site and a results report. Foot capture runs on the employee's own smartphone during a break, with no measurement day.
