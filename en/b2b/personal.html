How it works

Define the test group

You decide which 10 employees get the highest added value from new insoles as a test.

Foot capture

The participants photograph their feet with their own smartphone, for example during a break. There is no extra measurement day and no big effort.

Insoles are delivered

The insoles are 3D-printed locally in Germany and made within 48 hours. At most 7 days after the foot capture, they reach your site. The insoles can then be used in shoes your employees already own.

Results report

Together we survey the employees and can make the effect measurable. We ask about wear rate, perception and recommendation rates.

Roll out further

After positive feedback, you decide whether other employees or other sites should now be equipped with insoles.

Frequently asked questions

The essentials

How does a company introduce a new employee benefit such as insoles without it fizzling out?

A company can first introduce a new benefit with a limited test group and then decide whether and how to expand it. At VIANU, that is the pilot with 10 employees, and the results report shows at the end whether your team wears the insole.

How can a company tell whether an employee benefit is landing with the team?

A company can ask participants about their experience. At VIANU, you get a report at the end of the pilot on how helpful the insoles were and how satisfied the employees are with the measure. You can also show it to management.

How much effort does an insole pilot mean for the HR team?

The effort for the HR department in an insole pilot is manageable. With VIANU it is a few hours: you define the test group, approach the participants and briefly explain the process.

What happens in a pilot if only part of the team takes part?

A pilot does not need the whole team. At VIANU, participation is always voluntary and nobody is pushed into it. Anyone who can imagine that the insole adds value can take part. For a meaningful result, the group should ideally be filled up to 10 people.

More questions

Which employees can take part in an insole program?

An insole program suits all employees who stand or walk for many hours a day. The focus is on work in hotels and restaurants, such as kitchen, service, housekeeping, reception, banquet and bar. All other industries with a lot of standing and walking are just as welcome, for example care, retail, production, logistics and cleaning. At VIANU, participation is voluntary, and your company decides whether to take part.

How does a foot scan with a smartphone work?

In a foot scan with a smartphone, the feet are photographed with your own phone. No measurement day and no additional devices are needed. VIANU calls this foot capture: it takes place for example during a break, with no measurement day and no extra devices.

What happens if employees are sick or on vacation on the day of the foot scan?

The foot capture also works with the smartphone from anywhere. Anyone who is sick or on vacation simply does it later, once they are well again or back on site. The insoles are then delivered as normal.

What is VIANU?

VIANU is a program for teams whose staff are on their feet for many hours a day. Employees get custom-made insoles. VIANU is a comfort and appreciation offer that starts as a limited pilot. It consists of an approval package, implementation on site and a results report. Foot capture runs on the employee's own smartphone, for example during a break, with no measurement day.

How long does it take from the foot scan to delivery of the insoles?

How long it takes from the foot capture to delivery depends on the provider. At VIANU, the insole reaches your employees at the latest 7 days after the foot capture.

How long does an insole last in everyday work?

How long an insole lasts in everyday work depends on the provider. At VIANU, we know from our own tests that the insole holds up in perfect condition for at least 3 years.
