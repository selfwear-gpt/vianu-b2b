    No data yet

The pilot counts as a success if taking part is voluntary and free of pressure, no complaint reaches the works council, the employer sees only what the agreement allows, and the works agreement is signed before the start.

These are values on comfort and acceptance. They say nothing about sick days, turnover, performance or pain.

Data and data protection

This is how the foot capture works, in plain words. What is settled is shown with a solid line. What is still open is dashed and will only come with the data protection concept.

  1. Employees

    Take the photos of their feet with their own smartphone, during a break. Taking part is voluntary.

  2. Open

    Where the photos are processed, who can see them and how long they are stored.

  3. VIANU

    Makes the custom-made insole.

  4. Your company

    Receives the insoles for its employees and an anonymized results report at group level.

  5. Open

    What your company learns about the participation of individual people. There is no statement on this yet.

The essentials

Is taking part in an insole program voluntary?

Taking part in an insole program can be voluntary. At VIANU it is voluntary, and each person decides for themselves.

Is the performance of individual employees evaluated?

At the end of an insole pilot, participants report on comfort and acceptance. Performance is not measured. The results report is anonymized and shows the group. How VIANU treats individual answers is only settled with the data protection concept.

How does a foot scan with a smartphone work?

For a foot scan with a smartphone, the feet are photographed with your own phone. A measurement day and extra devices are not needed. VIANU calls this foot capture. It takes place during a break, and the details of the process are not settled yet.

What is VIANU?

VIANU is a program for teams whose staff are on their feet for many hours a day. Employees get custom-made insoles. It consists of an approval package, the rollout at your business and a results report. The foot capture runs on the employee's own smartphone during a break, with no measurement day.
