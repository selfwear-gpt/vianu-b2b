Is the performance of individual employees evaluated?

At the end of an insole pilot, participants report on comfort and acceptance. Performance is not measured. The results report is anonymized and shows the group. How VIANU treats individual answers is only settled with the data protection concept.

How does a foot scan with a smartphone work?

For a foot scan with a smartphone, the feet are photographed with your own phone. A measurement day and extra devices are not needed. VIANU calls this foot capture. It takes place during a break, with no measurement day and no extra devices.

What is VIANU?

VIANU is a program for teams whose staff are on their feet for many hours a day. Employees get custom-made insoles. It consists of an approval package, the rollout at your business and a results report. The foot capture runs on the employee's own smartphone during a break, with no measurement day.

How long does it take from the foot scan to delivery of the insoles?

That depends on the provider. At VIANU, the insole reaches your employees at the latest 7 days after the foot capture.

How long does an insole last in everyday work?

That depends on the provider. At VIANU, we know from our own tests that the insole holds up in perfect condition for at least 3 years.
