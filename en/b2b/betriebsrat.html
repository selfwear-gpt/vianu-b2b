Frequently asked questions

The essentials

Is taking part in an insole program voluntary?

Taking part in an insole program can be voluntary. At VIANU it is voluntary, and each person decides for themselves.

How does the foot scan work for employees?

Employees open our application on their smartphone in any browser. They take photos of their feet and of the current inner sole of their shoe, each next to an A4 sheet. Our patent-backed generative AI then supports anatomy recognition and the individual modeling of the insole. For the method of measuring feet from photos, patent DE 10 2019 123 458 B4 has been granted in Germany.

Who decides on taking part in an insole program?

Each person can decide for themselves whether to take part in an insole program if the offer is voluntary. Whether a company offers such a program is decided by the employer who pays for it. At VIANU, taking part is voluntary, for every person.

What happens to employee data during a foot scan for insoles?

During a foot scan, employees photograph their feet voluntarily with their own smartphone. At VIANU, the photos are processed in the EU, used only to make the insole and deleted afterwards. VIANU and its manufacturing partners can see them.

Does the employer see who takes part in an insole pilot?

Whether an employer sees who takes part depends on the program. At VIANU, the business knows the participant list, because it receives the insoles for these people. It sees the answers of the survey only as an anonymized report at group level, never individually.

Is the performance of individual employees evaluated?

At the end of an insole pilot, participants report on comfort and acceptance. Performance is not measured. The results report is anonymized and shows the group. How VIANU treats individual answers is only settled with the data protection concept.

More questions

Does an insole program need a works agreement?

Whether an insole program needs a works agreement is for the company and the works council to settle in each case, and this is not legal advice. VIANU offers a template for the works agreement that serves as a starting text for the negotiation. It is available as a file to download.

How is a custom insole made?

A custom insole can be made with 3D printing from the photos of the feet. At VIANU, it is printed locally in Germany and made within 48 hours. It reaches your employees at the latest 7 days after the foot capture.

How long are photos from a foot scan stored?

How long photos stay stored depends on the provider. At VIANU, they are used only to make the insole and deleted after production. The insole is printed within 48 hours.

How does a foot scan with a smartphone work?

For a foot scan with a smartphone, the feet are photographed with your own phone. A measurement day and extra devices are not needed. VIANU calls this foot capture. It takes place for example during a break, with no measurement day and no extra devices.

What is VIANU?

VIANU is a program for teams whose staff are on their feet for many hours a day. Employees get custom-made insoles. VIANU is a comfort and appreciation offer that starts as a limited pilot. It consists of an approval package, the rollout at your business and a results report. The foot capture runs on the employee's own smartphone, for example during a break, with no measurement day. For the method of measuring feet from photos, patent DE 10 2019 123 458 B4 has been granted in Germany.

How long does it take from the foot scan to delivery of the insoles?

How long it takes from the foot capture to delivery depends on the provider. At VIANU, the insole reaches your employees at the latest 7 days after the foot capture.

How long does an insole last in everyday work?

How long an insole lasts in everyday work depends on the provider. At VIANU, we know from our own tests that the insole holds up in perfect condition for at least 3 years.
