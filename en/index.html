    Questions you may have

    Insoles on the job

    What is a custom-made insole?

    A custom-made insole is an insole made for the foot of one person and placed in shoes that person already owns. VIANU makes insoles like this for teams whose staff are on their feet for many hours a day.

    Can my name be printed on the insole?

    Whether insoles can be printed with your name depends on the provider. At VIANU the name and company logo are printed on the insole.

    How long does an insole last in everyday work?

    How long an insole lasts in everyday work depends on the provider. At VIANU we know from our own tests that the insole lasts at least 3 years in perfect condition.

    How long does delivery take?

    How long it takes from the foot scan to delivery depends on the provider. At VIANU the insole reaches you within 7 days of the foot scan at the latest, at your workplace.

    Who is behind VIANU?

    VIANU is led by its founder Roderich Körner-Rehn. He himself stood on hard floors for 10 to 12 hours a day. The insoles are 3D-printed locally in Germany. For the method of measuring feet from photos, patent DE 10 2019 123 458 B4 has been granted in Germany. Your employer pays, and the satisfaction guarantee applies to all insoles.

    What happens if my insole does not fit?

    At VIANU all insoles come with the satisfaction guarantee. If your insole does not fit or you are not satisfied, we talk to you personally online and rework the insole or make a new one. It goes to your company free of charge. If you are still not satisfied afterwards, the purchase price is refunded in full.

    How does the foot scan work for me?

    You open our application on your smartphone in any browser. You take photos of your feet and of the current inner sole of your shoe, each next to an A4 sheet. Each foot capture needs 6 photos. Our patent-backed generative AI then supports anatomy recognition and the individual modeling of your insole. For the method of measuring feet from photos, patent DE 10 2019 123 458 B4 has been granted in Germany.

    Do insoles help with long hours of standing and walking at work?

    A review of 10 studies (Speed et al. 2018, Applied Ergonomics) found good evidence that cushioned materials, such as softer floors and cushioned insoles, go along with less discomfort and fatigue during long periods of standing at work.

    How is a custom insole made?

    A custom insole can be made with 3D printing from the photos of your feet. At VIANU, it is printed locally in Germany and made within 48 hours. It reaches you at the latest 7 days after the foot scan.

    How does a foot scan with a smartphone work?

    In a foot scan with a smartphone, the feet are photographed with your own phone. VIANU calls this foot capture: it takes place for example during a break, with no measurement day and no extra devices.

    Do you have to pay for work insoles yourself?

    Whether employees pay for work insoles themselves depends on the employer. With VIANU, your employer pays for the program, and you have no costs.

    Does an insole fit into work shoes you already have?

    An insole is placed in shoes you already own. You do not need new shoes for it.

    About VIANU

    What is VIANU?

    VIANU is a program for teams whose staff are on their feet for many hours a day. Employees get custom-made insoles. The employer signs the team up, foot capture takes place with your own smartphone, for example during a break, and the insole reaches the employees at the workplace. For the method of measuring feet from photos, patent DE 10 2019 123 458 B4 has been granted in Germany.

    Do I have to take part in VIANU?

    No, taking part in VIANU is voluntary. You decide whether you join.

    Who sees my photos during the foot scan?

    Your photos are processed in the EU, used only to make your insole and deleted after production. VIANU and its manufacturing partners can see them. Your employer knows the list of participants. It sees the survey answers only anonymously at group level.

    What happens if I am sick or on vacation on the day of the foot scan?

    The foot capture also works with your smartphone from anywhere. If you are sick or on vacation, you simply do it later, once you are well again or back. Your insoles are then delivered as normal.

    Can employees from the kitchen, service, housekeeping or other jobs with a lot of standing take part?

    Yes. Kitchen, service, housekeeping, reception and bar come first. All other jobs with a lot of standing and walking are meant too, for example care, retail, production, logistics and cleaning. Your employer decides whether your workplace takes part.

    How is the fit of my insole ensured, and how long does production take?

    With the high-precision 3D models from our technology partner SELFWEAR AI, we make your highly individual insole by 3D printing. Throughout the process, our foot experts naturally carry out further analysis and quality control to ensure the highest fit accuracy. It is made within 48 hours and reaches you at the latest 7 days after the foot scan.
