What this site does and does not do

  • This site sets no cookies.
  • This site uses no analytics or tracking services.
  • Fonts, scripts and images load from the site's own server. Loading the site makes no connection to third-party servers.
  • The name you type into the name insole stays in your browser and is not sent.
  • The buttons for copying a link or text use only the clipboard of your device.
  • Links that employees pass on to their employer contain the addition quelle=mitarbeitende. It contains no details about people, and the site does not evaluate it.
  • The pilot form does not send your details yet. It checks them in the browser and shows them as text that you can copy.
  • When you click a link to a study, you leave this site. The rules of the other site apply there.
